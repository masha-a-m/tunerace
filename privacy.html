Tune Race – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What we collect

Tune Race has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email, phone number or location. The nickname you enter, your scores and your play statistics are saved only on your own device and are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time from the Profile screen (Reset my stats), or by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it.

Music and third-party services

Song titles, artist names and 30-second preview clips are loaded directly from Deezer. When your device contacts Deezer, Deezer may receive technical information such as your IP address, under Deezer’s own privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may likewise receive your IP address. Tune Race is not affiliated with or endorsed by Deezer.

Advertising and purchases

Tune Race currently shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. If this changes, this policy will be updated first.

Children

Tune Race is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
